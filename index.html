How to install

Quick steps for each type of download.

androidAndroid apps (APK)

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open it from your notifications or the Downloads app.
  3. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager.
  4. Tap Install. For Tide, press Home and choose Tide as your default launcher.

usbNava OS (ISO)

  1. Download the ISO and get a USB stick of 4 GB or more (it will be erased).
  2. Write the ISO with balenaEtcher or Rufus (DD mode), or dd on Linux.
  3. Restart your PC, open its boot menu and choose the USB stick.
  4. Nava starts live. Live user: tekno, password 0000.
  5. To keep it, open the launcher and choose Install Nava OS. The disk you pick is erased.

desktop_windowsTry Nava OS in a VM

  1. Use QEMU/KVM, VirtualBox or VMware with 4 GB RAM and 2+ CPU cores.
  2. Turn on 3D acceleration: the desktop needs a GPU, even a virtual one.
  3. Attach the ISO as a CD and boot.
